Growing in popularity due to the recent rise of the promotion of ONE FC, Muay Thai is considered by many to be the absolute peak of striking martial arts. Unlike traditional western boxing which is restricted by two points of contact, or Muay Thai's sister system kickboxing with its four points of contact, the "art of eight limbs" utilizes one's hands, legs, knees, and elbows in competition. It is also unique in its devastating roundhouse kick that differs from the likes of Taekwondo or Karate, opting to strike with the shin bone rather than the foot.

Originating in Thailand, Muay Thai is deeply rooted in tradition and ritual with fighters performing the Wai Kru (a ritual dance) to pay respect to their teachers and ancestors; a ritual still performed today. Historians note that the system derived from the training of unarmed combat used by soldiers in the 13th-18th century. Today, many MMA fighters choose to study Muay Thai as their base of striking due to its effectiveness when on the feet.
